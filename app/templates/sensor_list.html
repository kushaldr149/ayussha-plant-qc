Sending readings from the plant system

The plant API must be switched on (PLANT_API_TOKEN in the .env file). A PLC, gateway or data logger then sends POST /api/v1/sensor-readings with the header Authorization: Bearer <token> and a body such as {"readings": [{"tag": "AG-MW-01", "value": 4.2}]}. Add "ref": "CB-20261022-001" to attach a reading to a coating or grinding batch, bag or site mix; readings without it are matched to batches and site mixes by their start and finish times. Readings are checked against each sensor's limits when saved. Field details are on /api/docs.
