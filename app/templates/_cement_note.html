PPC and PSC limits were read from IS 1489 (Part 1):2015 and IS 455:2015 with their amendments. The BIS text of IS 269:2015 (OPC 43 and 53) was not available; its limits are from the Indian Railways CAMTECH pamphlet on IS 269:2015 and were checked against the IS 269:2015 requirement columns of manufacturer test certificates. Check them against your copy of the standard. Every value can be changed afterwards in Setup.
